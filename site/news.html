What a SWAMITRA press release contains

Every release carries a dated headline, the Foundation's formal statement, the factual basis for it, and the name of the framework, study or programme it concerns. Where a figure is quoted, the release states where the figure comes from and over what period it was collected.

Releases also state clearly what the Foundation is not claiming. We do not attribute outcomes to our work without evidence, and we do not describe pilot activity as national coverage. Where a statement corrects an earlier one, the correction is marked and the original remains available.

Each release ends with an attribution line and media contact details. Journalists may quote any release in full without prior permission, provided the Foundation is named as the source.

Press release PDFs are hosted in the Resource Centre. Where a release accompanies a framework or study, the underlying document is published alongside it.

Contact Media Team

Working on a story?

Tell us your outlet, your subject and your deadline. We will tell you honestly whether we can help, and by when.

  • Interviews with programme leads on legal education, school safety, workplace dignity and child protection
  • Data and methodology notes behind any published figure
  • Review copies of frameworks, standards and research reports
  • Fact-checking support on constitutional and legal literacy claims

We do not offer comment on individual cases, ongoing inquiries or matters before a court or statutory authority. Where a question falls outside our competence, we will say so and, where we can, point you to someone better placed to answer it.

If you are working to a fixed publication time, tell us the date and we will prioritise accordingly.

Media enquiries are acknowledged within one working day. Details you send are used only to respond to your enquiry — see our Privacy Policy.
